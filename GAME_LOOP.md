# Core Game Loop

1. Start the workday at **9:00 AM**.
2. Receive tasks from emails, coworkers, the boss, and office equipment.
3. Walk around the office and complete tasks.
4. Every task consumes time and increases your stress.
5. Decide how to respond:
   - Do the task
   - Delay it
   - Take a short break
   - Say no
6. As the day progresses, interruptions become more frequent.
7. Reach **5:00 PM**.
8. Final event: the boss says, **"Before you leave... can you bring me a straw?"**
9. The player's accumulated stress determines the ending:
   - **Below the critical point:** The player calmly brings the straw and leaves for the day.
   - **Above the critical point:** The player snaps at the boss. This is the **critical point**—the emotional breaking point after a full day of accumulated stress.

## Gameplay Loop

**Receive → Prioritize → Act → Accumulate Stress → Recover (or Don't) → Repeat**
