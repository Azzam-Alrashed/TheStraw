# The Straw

> *that broke the camel's back*

A short narrative game about stress, pressure, and the invisible moment when **one small thing becomes too much**.

Created for the **SOP Game Jam 2026**.

---

## Theme

**Critical Point**

The game explores the idea behind the phrase:

> *"The straw that broke the camel's back."*

Every interruption seems insignificant on its own.

One email.
One meeting.
One phone call.
One "quick favor."

Eventually, a single, ordinary request becomes the **critical point**.

---

## Gameplay

You are an office worker trying to make it through the workday.

Throughout the day you will:

- Reply to emails
- Attend meetings
- Complete tasks
- Fix office problems
- Handle interruptions
- Manage your stress

Your objective is simple:

**Make it to the end of the day.**

---

## Features

- Pixel art office environment
- Dynamic stress system
- Increasing workplace pressure
- Short narrative experience
- Multiple office interactions
